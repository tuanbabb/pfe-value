3PFE Value table — for T + H to build the package

Five pillars. Each row = the value, the proof points we can stand behind today, and the customer-facing pitch we'd say in a Plus merchant call.

1. Done-with-you CRO
Strategy + execution, not just a tool.
Proof points
  • Dedicated CRO consultant (Shopify Partner, top-tier track record)
  • Quarterly experiment roadmap tied to merchant KPIs
  • Build queue: PageFly team ships pages, merchant reviews
Customer pitch

"You don't need another tool — you need a team that knows Shopify CRO and treats your funnel like their own. PFE gives you both: PageFly + a CRO partner."

2. AI leverage at enterprise scale
10K+ AI credits + early access to SmartSeller, behavioral triggers.
Proof points
  • 10,000+ AI credits/mo (vs. 4,000 on Power)
  • Early access to AI SmartSeller (Upcoming) + behavioral triggers
  • Smart pages priority queue + custom prompts
Customer pitch

"Your team already runs lean. PFE's AI budget means PageFly drafts the variants, runs the diagnostics, and surfaces winners — your CRO lead just approves and ships."

3. Multi-store + multi-region
Single contract, central design system, cross-store templates.
Proof points
  • Multi-store license (1 contract, N stores)
  • Shared global styles + custom blocks across stores
  • Localized template variants for region rollouts
Customer pitch

"Stop paying $199 × N stores and rebuilding the same hero five times. PFE gives you one license, one design system, and pages that propagate across regions."

4. Speed + reliability SLA
Dedicated CSM, response SLA, priority bug & feature queue.
Proof points
  • Dedicated CSM (1:N capped) — not shared chat queue
  • Response SLA: P1 < 2h, P2 < 8h business hours
  • Priority routing for bugs + feature requests
  • Quarterly business review with Tony / Head of Growth
Customer pitch

"At your scale, a 24h ticket loop costs more than the subscription. PFE puts you on a named CSM with an SLA you can take to your CFO."

5. Compounding ROI
Pricing built on lift, not seat count.
Proof points
  • Pricing tied to GMV band — fair on both sides
  • Quarterly lift report: experiments shipped × incremental CVR
  • Case studies: PageFly customers with documented +X% CVR
Customer pitch

"PFE pays for itself if we lift CVR by even 0.3 points on your top three pages. We share the receipt every quarter — if the lift isn't there, the package shouldn't be either."

4Key PFE concepts — what each term actually means

Concepts locked from PFE-CS-Flow-Proposal-v0.2 — the ones T + H need to be fluent in for sales calls. Memorize the ladder, the "say it" line is the customer-ready version.

Founder partnership

T's time becomes a strategic asset that escalates by tier — not a feature SKU.

Scale $2991 booking/mo · 45 min async store review từ T (email + Loom back)
Dominate $4992 bookings/mo · same async format
EmpireStrategic relationship: configurable cadence, co-author roadmap, QBR, network intros, Tech Lead + Founder committed
Say it to a Plus merchant

"At Empire, you're not buying booking slots — you're getting a co-founder who treats your funnel like ours. We map strategy, intro you to our partner network, and a Tech Lead owns your queue."

Per-store add-on · $50–100/mo each

Empire-only pricing model — linear by store count once you cross 3 stores.

Scale $2991 store · 1,600 credits/mo
Dominate $499Up to 3 stores bundled · 1,600 credits per store (separate pools)
EmpireBase custom + $50–100/mo per additional store. Each add-on has its own 1,600-credit pool. Range gives volume discount: 5 stores ≈ $100/store, 30 stores ≈ $50/store
Say it to a Plus merchant

"Add a store, add a credit pool. The 6th store doesn't share credits with the 1st — each one stands on its own. As volume grows, per-store cost drops."

White-glove onboarding & training

Empire-only — handhold the merchant team to "first value" instead of self-serve docs.

ScaleSelf-serve onboarding + 24/7 chat support
DominateNamed PIC + ASAP queue · 1:1 setup help
EmpireDedicated kick-off (PageFly team on calls) · custom training sessions for merchant's design + dev team · documented playbook for their stores · ongoing knowledge transfer
Say it to a Plus merchant

"We don't drop you into docs. We run the kick-off, train your team on your own stack, build the playbook in your tone, and stay on calls until your team owns it."

Note · "Custom contract + invoice billing" intentionally not in Empire scope yet — billing rails not built. Revisit in v2 once procurement/finance side ready.
